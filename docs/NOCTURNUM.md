# Nocturnum

Nocturnum is the public CrossingKey visual system.

## Palette

- Void Black: `#050508`
- Deep Panel: `#0b0b12`
- Soft Silver: `#d7d7df`
- Atomic Hot Magenta: `#ff2bd6`

The accent is intentionally scarce. Large uninterrupted magenta surfaces are discouraged unless a specific product context requires them.

## Composition

Use deep negative space, decisive alignment, compact information density, and strong typographic hierarchy. Chrome, silver, or engraved visual references may be used as controlled texture, not as substitutes for information architecture.

## Typography

CrossingKey favors technical, geometric display typography where it improves identity, paired with highly readable body text. Typography must remain usable at small sizes and across mobile layouts.

## UI behavior

Surfaces should communicate state before spectacle. Loading, failure, degraded behavior, permissions, irreversible actions, and completion should be visually distinct.

## Accessibility

Color is never the sole carrier of meaning. Interactive elements require visible focus, usable hit targets, semantic labels, and sensible reduced-motion behavior.
